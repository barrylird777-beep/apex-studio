# Bible Film Production

Apex Studio is a general cinematic production studio, not a Korn-only video generator.

Supported production domains:
- `bible` — Scripture-based films, episodes, shorts, documentaries, explainers, and other Bible media.
- `korn` — Garden of Apex / Jesus Freaks productions.
- `original` — original non-Bible, non-Garden productions.

## Bible mode

Bible productions use Scripture/source provenance as their truth boundary. They can use Scripture research, historical/context research, character and scene planning, original cinematic visualization, Music Radar audio, deterministic render QC, durable PostgreSQL production jobs, and release packaging.

Bible mode does not require Garden of Apex references.

## Korn mode

Korn productions use the verified Garden graph/package and its environmental rules. Unverified Garden references are rejected before production handoff.

## Studio rule

**Apex Studio is capable of making Bible movies and videos as a first-class production domain. Korn content is one domain, not the definition of the Studio.**
