# PlanetApeX Historical Research — Manuscript Discovery Addendum

**Updated:** 2026-10-13  
**Purpose:** extend the recovered research trail with source-grounded, directly reachable manuscript collections.  
**Evidence policy:** separate catalog facts from interpretation. A digital catalog record is not proof that a text has been transcribed, collated, or imported into PlanetApeX. Keep the original locator and rights statement attached to every later record.  
**Scope:** research documentation only. No runtime, production, storage, or existing archive records changed.

## Status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED CATALOG RECORD** — the named institution's own record directly supports the metadata stated here.
- **VERIFIED COLLECTION / DISCOVERY PORTAL** — the institution describes a searchable collection; this does not verify every individual item.
- **FOLLOW-UP LEAD** — a concrete next query generated from an official catalog or collection.
- **NOT YET VERIFIED** — text transcription, textual relationship, image reuse rights beyond the institution's statement, or import into Apex has not been checked.

## B. Dead Sea Scrolls — searchable primary-source image archive

- **Official portal:** [Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library — Explore the Archive](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive?locale=en_US)
- **Verified portal capabilities:** free online archive; search by item ID or text; browse by archaeological site, language, and content; high-definition spectral images and older infrared images are described by the portal.
- **Portal-reported browse counts at research time:** Qumran 913; Wadi Murabba'at 159; Nahal Hever 112; Masada 47. Language facets include Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek and others; content facets include biblical compositions, non-biblical compositions, documents, letters, and unidentified texts.
- **Status:** **VERIFIED COLLECTION / DISCOVERY PORTAL.** Counts are portal facets and may change. They are not a count of distinct complete scrolls or independent works.
- **Next action:** record item-level identifiers and image/description URLs for selected biblical compositions and non-biblical witnesses. Capture cave/site, language, material/text layer when stated, catalog label, and any uncertainty in identification. Do not infer a manuscript's contents from a broad category alone.

## C. Cairo Geniza — Jewish, Christian, and Mediterranean documentary context

- **Official collection overview:** [Cambridge University Library — Taylor-Schechter Genizah Research Unit](https://www.lib.cam.ac.uk/collections/departments/taylor-schechter-genizah-research-unit)
- **Official collection history:** [Taylor-Schechter Genizah Collection](https://www.lib.cam.ac.uk/collections/departments/taylor-schechter-genizah-research-unit/taylor-schechter-genizah-priceless)
- **Verified collection facts:** Cambridge describes approximately 193,000 manuscript fragments, chiefly in Hebrew, Judaeo-Arabic, Aramaic and Arabic, spanning religious/literary texts as well as letters, legal documents, and records of daily life. The unit states that high-resolution images and descriptions for more than 21,000 fragments are available through Cambridge Digital Library, with new fragments added.
- **Status:** **VERIFIED COLLECTION / DISCOVERY PORTAL.** The collection-wide totals do not mean every fragment is digitized or open for unrestricted image reuse.
- **Next action:** prioritize Bible-related fragments, biblical commentaries, translations, liturgical material, and dated documentary context. Store the fragment's own catalog identifier and rights/permissions statement; do not copy images into Apex merely because metadata is visible.

## D. Sinai Palimpsests and the Sinai Manuscripts Data Portal

- **Project overview:** [Sinai Palimpsests Project](https://sinaied.library.ucla.edu/about-project/index.html)
- **Structured catalog portal:** [Sinai Manuscripts Data Portal](https://sinaiportal.library.ucla.edu/)
- **Verified project facts:** the project describes more than 160 known palimpsests at St Catherine's and spectral-imaging work to recover erased undertexts. The portal separates records into manuscript objects, inscribed layers, and text units, and includes authority records for works and agents.
- **Status:** **VERIFIED COLLECTION / DISCOVERY PORTAL.** The project reports texts in at least ten languages. A manuscript object, an inscribed layer, and a text unit are different entities and must not be flattened into one record.
- **Next action:** use the portal's data model for layered witnesses. Record overtext and undertext independently; preserve script/language, confidence of identification, folio locator, and source edition. Treat reconstructed readings as scholarly interpretations, not direct image facts.

## E. Coptic SCRIPTORIUM — searchable texts and linguistic data

- **Corpus index:** [Coptic SCRIPTORIUM — Corpora](https://data.copticscriptorium.org/index/corpus/)
- **Search interface:** [Coptic SCRIPTORIUM Search](https://data.copticscriptorium.org/search/)
- **Public corpus repository and formats:** [CopticScriptorium/corpora](https://github.com/CopticScriptorium/corpora)
- **Verified capabilities:** browse and search Coptic works, use stable CTS URNs, and access corpus data in formats including TEI XML, PAULA XML, and CoNLL-U. The repository documents annotation metadata and item-level licensing exceptions.
- **Status:** **VERIFIED COLLECTION / DISCOVERY PORTAL.** Searchable text and linguistic annotations are not identical to a full photographic manuscript facsimile. Parallel witnesses may differ; the corpus documentation explicitly warns that some are not text-identical.
- **Next action:** identify biblical and early-Christian works by URN; preserve edition, dialect, witness relationship, annotation level, version, and license. Do not treat automated linguistic annotation as a verified critical edition.

## Cross-collection ingestion rules

1. Keep the source institution's stable URL and catalog identifier as the primary external locator.
2. Separate manuscript object, physical fragment/folio, inscribed layer, textual witness, work/title, edition/transcription, and translation into linked but distinct records.
3. Use evidence labels **KNOWN**, **OBSERVED**, **INFERRED**, and **UNKNOWN**. For example, “catalog says dated 897” is a catalog-reported fact; a proposed textual relationship is an inference until supported by collation.
4. Track rights at the level the source actually states. LOC's public-domain statement for its Sinai microfilm collection does not establish reuse rights for every other institution's images.
5. Deduplicate on stable source identifiers first; use title similarity only to suggest possible duplicates, never to merge witnesses automatically.
6. Record access separately from existence: a cataloged item, an image set, a transcription, a licensed text, and an Apex-imported artifact are distinct states.
7. Do not claim the previous discovery queue's full count has increased until entries are actually added to and deduplicated against the canonical queue. This addendum adds **seven directly checked LOC item records and four collection-level research paths**; it does not claim a full archive import.
