# THE BIBLICALLY SEEN

THE BIBLICALLY SEEN is the flagship creative universe built on Apex Studio.

## Source discipline
The system intentionally separates:

- **Scripture** — what a selected biblical text actually contains.
- **Ancient source** — what another historical/ancient work says.
- **Tradition** — teachings or practices associated with a religious tradition.
- **Interpretation** — an argument or reading of a source.
- **Evidence** — independently documented historical, archaeological, or scholarly material.
- **Speculation** — an unverified proposal.
- **Original fiction** — material created for the universe.

These labels are metadata, not theological judgments. A source may be important without being treated as equivalent to Scripture, and a generated scene may draw from multiple traditions while retaining its provenance.

## Coverage map
The initial taxonomy supports Scripture, Deuterocanonical literature, Second Temple and Enochic literature, Catholic and Orthodox traditions, Jewish traditions, Gnostic texts, ancient Near Eastern context, church history, archaeology, scholarship, mystical/esoteric historical literature, modern interpretation, and original fiction.

## Creative rule
Go deep, but never silently turn an interpretation into a quotation, a tradition into a historical fact, or generated fiction into a source claim.
